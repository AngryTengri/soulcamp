FAQ

When and where is it?

November 21–22 in Almaty. The venue will be announced soon.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to join. Included: food, drinks, AI tools, travel and accommodation.

Who can apply?

Girls aged 18+.

I don’t work in the creative industry. Can I apply?

Yes. We care about your vision, taste and ideas, not your job title or resume.

I don’t live in Almaty. Can I join?

Yes, participants from other cities and countries are welcome. [Placeholder — confirm travel and accommodation details.]

Do I need a portfolio?

No, but you can add links to your work.

I don’t have a team. Can I participate?

Yes! You can participate solo or be matched with other participants to form a team.

Can I apply with a friend? What is the maximum team size?

Yes. You can apply solo or in a team of up to 4 people. Every team member must submit their own registration form and list each other as collaborators so we can review your application together.

I’ve never used AI before. Can I participate?

Yes. You don’t need any prior technical experience with AI tools. The workshops and mentors on Day 1 will guide you through the tools you need to bring your concept to life.

When do applications close?

Main registration closes on Monday, November 16 at 11:59 PM. Official invites go out on November 18.

What if I’m not sure I can come on both days?

Tell us as early as you can, because spots are limited.
