QUESTIONS

When and where is it?

November 21–22 in Almaty. The venue will be announced soon.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to join. Included: food, drinks and AI tools.

What language is the event in?

Panels and workshops are in Russian and English. Mentors speak Russian, Kazakh, and English.

Can I stay at the venue overnight?

Yes. The venue stays open all night, with a chill zone if you need a break.

Who can apply?

Girls aged 18+.

I don’t work in the creative industry. Can I apply?

Yes. We care about your vision, taste and ideas, not your job title or resume.

I’ve never used AI before. Can I participate?

Yes. You don’t need any prior technical experience with AI tools. The workshops and mentors on Day 1 will guide you through the tools you need to bring your concept to life.

Do I need a portfolio?

No, but you can add links to your work.

I don’t live in Almaty. Can I join?

Yes, participants from other cities and countries are welcome.

Can I apply with a friend? What is the maximum team size?

Yes. You can apply solo or in a team of up to 4 people. Every team member must submit their own registration form and list each other as collaborators so we can review your application together.

I don’t have a team. Can I find one at camp?

Yes. Apply solo, and you can find teammates at registration or work on your own.

When do applications close?

We review applications on a rolling basis and send invites until November 20. Apply early, as spots are limited.

How will I know if I’m selected?

We’ll send your invite by email before November 20, with everything you need to know.

What if I’m not sure I can come on both days?

Tell us as early as you can, because spots are limited.

Do I get free access to Higgsfield, or do I need my own credits?

Every participant gets free Higgsfield credits for the weekend. You don’t need a subscription.

Can I use other AI tools?

Yes. You can use any AI or editing tools, as long as Higgsfield is part of your workflow.

Can I prepare anything before the brief drops?

You can learn the tools and form your team in advance, but all project work starts after the brief drops at 14:00 on Day 1.

How is the work judged, and who are the judges?

A jury of the Higgsfield team and industry professionals scores each project on idea, taste, and execution. Judges will be announced before camp.
